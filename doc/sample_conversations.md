# QuickBite Sample Conversations

The following examples show some of the main conversation flows supported by QuickBite.

## 1. Food Ordering

**User:** I want pizza  
**QuickBite:** Which pizza would you like? Margherita Pizza, Pepperoni Pizza, or Veggie Supreme Pizza?

**User:** Pepperoni  
**QuickBite:** How many Pepperoni Pizza would you like?

**User:** 2  
**QuickBite:** What is your delivery address?

**User:** 123 Main Street  
**QuickBite:** Please confirm your order: 2 x Pepperoni Pizza to 123 Main Street. Total: £20.98. Confirm?

**User:** Yes  
**QuickBite:** Order placed successfully! Your order ID is QB-1001.


## 2. Order Tracking and Cancellation

**User:** Track QB-1001  
**QuickBite:** Your order QB-1001 is currently preparing.

**User:** Cancel QB-1001  
**QuickBite:** Are you sure you want to cancel order QB-1001? Please confirm yes or no.

**User:** Yes  
**QuickBite:** Order QB-1001 has been cancelled successfully.


## 3. Invalid Dish Recovery

**QuickBite:** Sorry, I couldn't find "unicorn burger" on our menu. Here are today's options: Margherita Pizza, Pepperoni Pizza, Veggie Supreme Pizza, Classic Cheeseburger, BBQ Bacon Burger, Caesar Salad, Grilled Chicken Wrap, Chocolate Lava Cake. Which would you like?

**User:** BBQ  
**QuickBite:** How many BBQ Bacon Burger would you like?

**User:** 2  
**QuickBite:** What is your delivery address?

**User:** 123 Main Street  
**QuickBite:** Please confirm your order: 2 x BBQ Bacon Burger to 123 Main Street. Total: £19.98. Confirm?

**User:** Yes  
**QuickBite:** Order placed successfully! Your order ID is QB-1001.
